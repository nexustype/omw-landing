Privacy Policy

This page is a draft placeholder and should be reviewed before public legal reliance.

Data collected

When you submit a beta form, OMW may collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, route details, timing, use case, and optional notes.

Purpose

This information is used to understand beta demand, identify compatible route patterns, contact potential testers, and improve the OMW service.

Contact and retention

OMW keeps beta signup information only as long as needed for early testing and follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required by law or future consent.

Sharing

OMW does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by technical providers used to host the website, receive form submissions, or operate email communications.

Your rights

You may contact OMW to request access, correction, or deletion of your information at hello@rideomw.com.
